FLOODLENS · FPV

You're a drone over a Malaysian river valley during the monsoon. It's raining and cloudy, and the oil-palm estate looks fine from above. Swing your head to change what your eyes can see.

  • Swing right (E / flick right) → change vision: Optical → C-band radar → L-band NISAR
  • Swing left (Q / flick left) → go back
  • 1 · 2 · 3 or mouse wheel → jump between satellite passes (before / peak / receding)
  • WASD + drag → fly around

Prototype for look-and-feel only. Terrain, flood and numbers are simulated, not real NISAR measurements.

原型只用来确认交互感觉：右甩切换"人眼 → C 波段 → L 波段 NISAR"，看油棕园树下隐藏的洪水。
