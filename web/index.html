FLOODLENS

NISAR L-BAND · SEE THE FLOOD UNDER THE PALMS

You are a drone over a Malaysian river valley in the monsoon. It is raining, the sky is cloud, and the oil-palm estate looks fine from above. Swing your head to change which satellite's eyes you see with.

  • Swing right (E / flick right) → Optical → C-band radar → L-band NISAR
  • Swing left (Q / flick left) → go back
  • Number keys or mouse wheel → jump between satellite passes
  • WASD + drag → fly · X → NISAR lens

This build runs on SIMULATED stand-in data (terrain, flood, imagery and numbers are synthetic). Real NISAR, HLS and Sentinel-1 data replace it through the same files.
