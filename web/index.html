FloodLens

Fly a drone over a Malaysian flood zone in the monsoon. From above, the oil-palm estates look fine. Swing your head to borrow a different satellite's eyes and see what is really down there.

  1. Swing right with E or a quick flick: optical, then C-band radar, then L-band NISAR.
  2. Swing left with Q to go back.
  3. Number keys or the mouse wheel jump between satellite passes.
  4. WASD and drag to fly. X opens a lens onto another sensor.

This build runs on simulated stand-in data: terrain, flood, imagery and numbers are synthetic. Real NISAR, HLS and Sentinel-1 data replace it through the same files.
